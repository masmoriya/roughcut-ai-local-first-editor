PRD 17: OPEN SOURCE LICENSE, BRAND AND DISTRIBUTION DECISION
Priority: P0 decision before distributing modified binaries

KNOWN
RoughCut is GPL-3.0 licensed. Forking, inspecting and privately modifying source code is allowed subject to license. Distribution of modified GPL-covered binaries can carry corresponding-source, license notice and attribution obligations. This is a planning statement, not legal advice.

DECISION REQUIRED
Are we building (A) an open-source GPL-3.0 fork and extension, (B) a separate proprietary Resolve client that communicates at arm's length with a GPL app, or (C) a clean-room alternative? Do not assume separation by process/MCP alone resolves all derivative-work questions. Get qualified counsel before shipping commercial proprietary integration.

DEFAULT FOR MVP
Keep the fork clearly GPL-3.0, retain upstream notices and source availability, avoid integrating it into unrelated proprietary application code, and maintain separate product identity/branding. No license re-labeling.

REQUIREMENTS
LIC-01 Retain LICENSE, third-party notices, source provenance, upstream contributor attribution.
LIC-02 Record all source modifications and link a corresponding-source archive/commit for any distributed GPL binary.
LIC-03 Scan all Rust, Node, ffmpeg, whisper, model weights, fonts and runtime components for their own license conditions. Some model files have separate use restrictions.
LIC-04 Do not copy vendor trademarks/branding in a way implying affiliation.
LIC-05 Before shipping proprietary products: obtain legal advice on GPL coupling, IPC boundary, source obligations, patent clauses, installer obligations and distribution jurisdictions.
LIC-06 Separate license approvals from technical test success. Passing CI does not mean distribution is legally cleared.
LIC-07 Identify which hosted APIs, if any, have privacy terms and contractual data handling obligations.
